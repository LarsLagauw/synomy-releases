Connect your AI.

Synomy can turn a finished meeting into notes: the to-do's, the decisions, who said what. For that it needs an AI service. This takes five minutes, once, and you can start for free.

Step by step

The service is Ollama Cloud. You make an account there, it gives you a key, and you paste that key into Synomy. That's all.

  1. Make an Ollama account.

    Go to ollama.com and click Sign in at the top right. Sign up with your email address, or with Continue with Google or Continue with GitHub. If you used email, open the message Ollama sends you and confirm it.

    You don't need to download Ollama itself, and you don't need a credit card. The free plan is enough to start.

  2. Create a key.

    Signed in, go to ollama.com/settings/keys. Create a new key and give it a name you'll recognise, like Synomy. Copy the key it shows you.

    Treat the key like a password: anyone who has it can use your account. Don't send it to anyone. If it ever leaks, delete it on the same page and make a new one.

  3. Paste it into Synomy.

    In Synomy, click the gear in the top bar to open Settings, and go to AI. Paste the key into Ollama Cloud key, then click anywhere outside the field. It's saved on this computer only.

  4. Choose the model, or leave it empty.

    The Model field says which AI writes your notes. Leave it empty and Synomy uses gemma4:31b, which works on the free plan. To use another one, type its name exactly as below.

    Free plan · the default

    gemma4:31b

    Google's Gemma 4. Writes clear notes in English, Dutch, French, German and many other languages, and answers in a second or two.

    $0.14 read, $0.40 written, per million tokens

    Smarter, about the same price

    glm-5.3-flash

    Z.ai's GLM-5.3 Flash, from August 2026. Noticeably sharper at long, messy meetings. It thinks before it writes, so a summary takes longer.

    $0.15 read, $0.50 written · needs Pro or added credits

    Either way, an hour-long meeting costs around a cent or less. Prices are Ollama's, October 2026; they can change, and ollama.com/pricing has today's.

    The technical bit

    Synomy calls https://ollama.com/api/chat with your key and the model name you typed, so any name from Ollama's cloud list works, with or without -cloud at the end. Prices are per million tokens; a token is about three quarters of an English word.

    A free key that asks for a model outside the free plan gets “not in the Free plan” back, and the meeting simply has no Summary tab. GLM-5.3 Flash always reasons first, and that reasoning counts toward what it may write per pass, so on a very long meeting Gemma 4 can be the more dependable choice.

  5. Record a meeting.

    When the transcript is finished, the meeting gets a Summary tab with the notes. Nothing else changes: without a key, Synomy works exactly the same, just without that tab.

No Summary tab after a meeting?

Check these three, in this order:

The key. Paste it again in Settings › AI, without spaces, or make a new one on Ollama's keys page.

The model. Empty the Model field, so Synomy uses gemma4:31b. A name that is misspelled, retired, or not in your plan gives no summary.

Your free use. It comes back every month. For more, add credits or take Ollama's Pro plan ($20 a month, with $60 of use in it). You can see what you've used at ollama.com/settings.

What gets sent, and to whom?

Only the transcript of the meeting you recorded, once it's finished, and only when you've pasted a key. Not your other notes, not your pages, not your files. This is the one thing in Synomy that sends your own words to a company.

Ollama says it doesn't store your meetings or train on them. That's their promise, not ours, so read their privacy policy if it matters to you. You can delete the key in Synomy's Settings at any time, and nothing is sent after that.
