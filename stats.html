            How this is counted

            Once a day while it runs, Synomy sends a random number made on your computer, its version, which days it was used, and how many times each feature was. Never your notes, your name, the people you share with, or anything you typed, and the number is not your friend code. The counter keeps no addresses.

            Only totals are published, and anything fewer than five computers share is folded into "other" or left out. You can switch counting off in Settings › Diagnostics; that also deletes the number and the counts on your computer.
